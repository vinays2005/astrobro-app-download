Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

AstroBro ("we", "the app") provides Vedic astrology charts and AI-assisted readings. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who processes it.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password).
  • Birth details you enter: name, date, time and place of birth, including the latitude and longitude of that place. If you allow location access, the app may use your device location to help fill in a place.
  • Chat content: the questions you ask and the answers returned, saved so your history is available when you return.
  • Plan and usage: your plan, its expiry, payment and order identifiers, and your daily chat count.
  • Advertising data: free users may see ads from Google AdMob, which can use your device advertising ID.

How we use it

  • To calculate your charts and generate readings and PDF reports.
  • To enforce the free daily chat limit and activate paid plans.
  • To show ads to free users, and to keep the service secure and working.

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database storage of your profile, chat history and plan.
  • Our backend server (hosted on Railway) — receives your birth details and questions to compute charts and answers.
  • Groq — the AI model provider. Your question and the relevant chart details are sent to it to generate an answer.
  • Qdrant Cloud — a search index of astrology reference text; your question is used to look up relevant passages.
  • Razorpay — payments. Card, UPI and bank details go directly to Razorpay; we receive only a payment/order ID and status.
  • Google AdMob — ads for free users, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can clear your chat history inside the app. To request deletion of your account and data, contact us using the details below.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Firebase. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

AstroBro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Nature of the service

Astrology readings are for guidance and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
