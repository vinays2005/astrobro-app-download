Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

No subscription to cancel

AstroBro plans (Spark ₹49 / 7 days, Core ₹149 / 30 days, Pro ₹399 / 90 days, Elite ₹999 / 1 year) are one-time payments for a fixed period. They never renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. When a plan ends you simply return to the free tier.

Refunds

Because a plan unlocks digital features immediately, payments for an activated plan are generally non-refundable. We will refund or fix the following:

  • Payment taken but plan not activated — we will activate the plan or refund the payment.
  • Duplicate payment for the same plan — the extra payment is refunded.
  • Plan unusable because of a fault on our side that we cannot fix — a refund for the affected period.

How to ask

Contact us within 7 days of the payment and include your registered email and the Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_). Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually reach you within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
